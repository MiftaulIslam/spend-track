SpendTrack · design prototype
Design system & screen library

Ledger Instrument

SpendTrack's visual world: warm paper instead of white, hairline rules instead of card soup, monospace tabular numerals carrying every quantity, and brass used as a scarce signal — never as decoration. Built mobile-first, verified at 390 px and 1440 px.
